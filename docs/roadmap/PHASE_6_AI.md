### Phase 6 — AI, A2A and Multi-Property
Window: Months 16–18 (original target 2028-Q1; provisional). Goal: bounded AI and enterprise capability on reliable data and permissions.

## Work
- Start with multilingual concierge tasks: answer approved questions, check availability and prepare bookings.
- Require confirmation before committing/changing reservations unless venue explicitly configures a safe policy.
- Evaluate SevenRooms/PolyAI API as a lead; verify current API, authorization, data processing, supported flows and costs.
- Define A2A auth/capabilities, consent, rate limits, replay protection, audit, cancellation and escalation. No unrestricted VIP-profile access.
- Human handoff for uncertainty, unusual requests, complaints, accessibility needs and low confidence. Measure workload reduction/escalation; no 100% automation promise.
- Post-shift summaries use authorized data, source links, correction and role access.
- GEO is an experiment gated by measured value and accuracy.
- Contribution-margin views have traceable inputs/assumptions.
- Cross-property profiles are opt-in, purpose-limited and property-controlled; specify tenant isolation, residency, retention and deletion first.
- Multi-currency consolidation follows accounting/reconciliation requirements.

## Exit evidence
Test chosen languages/accents, noise, ambiguity, cancellation and handoff. Verify authorization on all agent reads/writes and audit them. Review recordings/transcripts, retention and vendor processing. Test tenant isolation/deletion with synthetic data.

Gate: AI reduces measured workload while preserving guest choice, staff control and tested fallback; cross-property sync has independent privacy approval.



## Technology fit update (supplementary; exact roadmap bullets above are unchanged)
- Build a permissioned booking service and vendor API integration before adding A2A. A2A is optional interoperability for independent agents, not booking authorization or a PMS connector.
- Start voice AI with a small set of demanded languages/intents, clear AI disclosure, booking confirmation, measured accuracy, and human fallback. EU AI Act transparency obligations for relevant direct interactions apply from 2 August 2026.
- Treat GEO as an optional marketing experiment; standard SEO and useful, accurate content remain the baseline.
- Define VIP data purposes, category-specific retention, tenant/property boundaries, access, residency and deletion propagation before global profile synchronization.
- Multi-currency reporting needs accounting ownership, FX source/timing, rounding and reconciliation before consolidation.
